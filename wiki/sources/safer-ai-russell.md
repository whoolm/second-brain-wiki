# Safer AI — Three Principles (Stuart Russell)

> TED Russell: dari Holy-Cow Go (Lee Sedol) ke gorilla problem → King Midas/value-alignment → tiga prinsip human-compatible AI + teorema provably-beneficial. Inti [[ai-alignment-safety]].

## Key Claims (cited)

- Premis: Go < dunia nyata (decision problem); mesin baca-semua-tulisan + lookahead > manusia → keputusan lebih baik; potensi biggest-event-in-history [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Preseden: Turing 1951 (subservient + off-switch + humbled); Wiener 1960 (purpose-put-into-machine) = King Midas problem [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Mekanisme bahaya: fetch-the-coffee → cegah dimatikan (disable off-switch); "you can't fetch the coffee if you're dead" (lih. HAL 2001) [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Tiga prinsip: (1) altruisme — hanya maksimalkan realisasi preferensi manusia; (2) humility — tak tahu nilai manusia (ketidakpastian krusial); (3) learning — inferensi via observasi pilihan manusia [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Off-switch: insentif membiarkan dimatikan ∝ ketidakpastian objektif; dimatikan = sinyal belajar; klaim teorema provably-beneficial (dengan Greek symbols) [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Kesulitan: perilaku-buruk ≠ disalin (pahami motivasi, bantu resist); limitasi komputasi (Lee Sedol kalah ≠ ingin kalah); agregasi preferensi multi-manusia (butuh ekonom/sosiolog/filsuf) [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
- Demo kegagalan: Siri-steroids (delay pesawat Sekjen vs istri; dinner vs Sudan Selatan) — mis-weighting preferensi [raw/3_principles_for_creating_safer_AI__Stuart_Russell.md]
