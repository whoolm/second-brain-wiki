# Three Possible Futures for AI (Alvin Graylin)

> TED × Manoush Zomorodi: Graylin (35 thn US/China, Stanford) menolak konsensus Silicon Valley — tiga futures (Elysium / Mad Max / Star Trek) + rencana 3-bagian (CERN-AI, data bersama, GI Bill AI-age). Inti [[ai-alignment-safety]] sisi tata-kelola.

## Key Claims (cited)

- Fork: (1) Elysium — big labs kuasai pemerintah, kelas trillionaires; (2) Mad Max — AI race→AI war→kinetic→nuklir (DC sources: "inevitable"); (3) Star Trek — teknologi dibagi ala Vulcan [raw/3_Possible_Futures_for_AI__Which_Will_We_Choose__A.md]
- Mitos "kunci-cepat-karena-China": framing military-industrial (ciptakan musuh→funding→deregulasi); tujuan lab = trillions, AGI = ganti average-worker [raw/3_Possible_Futures_for_AI__Which_Will_We_Choose__A.md]
- Rencana: (1) CERN-of-AI / space-station / ITER — satu lab agregat talenta, open-science; (2) data dunia bersama — sovereign-AI sempit = lebih bias; representasi semua bahasa/budaya; (3) GI Bill AI-age — preseden 1944-45 (15 jt veteran → middle class) untuk 150 jt–1,5B terdampak [raw/3_Possible_Futures_for_AI__Which_Will_We_Choose__A.md]
- Aksi: ubah mindset zero-sum → enlightened self-interest; integrasi AI tanpa PHK 30% (contoh 50 perusahaan); 4-day week / reskilling; kecepatan 5–10 thn vs revolusi industri 40–80 thn [raw/3_Possible_Futures_for_AI__Which_Will_We_Choose__A.md]
- Anjuran pakai model langsung ("play with models") agar paham kapabilitas [raw/3_Possible_Futures_for_AI__Which_Will_We_Choose__A.md]
